Mansionly: marks for private estates

The visual language of private banks, Swiss watchmakers and great estates: engraved lines, a crest, the estate gate, a signet seal. Each is shown on navy with gold and on ivory with navy, and at app-icon size.

E1 · The estate crestA heraldic shield holding a gable roof and the front door. Says “we guard this house” without saying security.
E2 · The estate gateWrought-iron gates between two stone pillars, gold finials. The first thing you see at any great estate, and the line we hold for the owner.
E3 · The signet sealA seal as on a family signet ring or a sealed letter: “Private Estate Stewards · California”.
E4 · The laurelA laurel wreath around the house: the classic sign of honour and trust, used by maisons for centuries.
E5 · The badgeA framed initial with a double border, like a coachbuilder’s badge on a fine car.
E6 · The keystone archAn engraved arch with the keystone that holds everything together. Quiet, architectural, permanent.
E7 · Crossed keysTwo keys crossed beneath a roof: the ancient sign of a trusted steward who keeps the keys of the house.
E8 · Engraved nameThe name alone, engraved between hairlines with the founding year in Roman numerals. Like a Swiss private bank.

Choose one or two. The final version gets hand-tuned line weights and spacing, a one-colour version for engraving, foil and embroidery, and the small icon for phone and browser.
