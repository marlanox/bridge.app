mansionly. brand book

One page that says how the brand looks, sounds and grows: the logo, the symbol, colours, type, slogan, the family of services, and exactly where each thing appears.

01 · The logo

Shield, bright m, quiet letters, period.

Why it works

The keyhole shield says “your home is protected, and we hold the key”. The bright m starts the name with confidence; the quieter letters keep it calm. The period closes it: a full stop, a promise kept.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the shield on every side. Nothing (text, photos, edges) comes closer.

Minimum size

Full logo: 110 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Smaller than that, use the shield on its own.

02 · The symbol

Logo vs app icon: two different jobs.

App icon · m. under the roof

The app on the phone, the browser tab, social profile pictures. White m, gold roof, gold period on navy.

Shield · inside the logo

The shield always stays part of the logo. On its own it is used only on the uniform chest, key tags and the seal on printed reports.

05 · Slogan and promise

Kept as if you never left.

The promise (one sentence under the slogan)

Owning a fine home is a full-time job. While you are away, we do it for you.

The explanation (one paragraph)

Mansionly looks after homes while their owners are away: weekly visits, a photo report after every one, mail and vendors handled, and one trusted team for everything else the house needs, from a gardener to the sale of the home.

Short lines for ads, cars and social

  • Your home. Our watch.
  • Someone is always home.
  • We hold the keys. You hold the peace of mind.
  • While you are away, we keep watch.

Spanish: “Cuidada como si nunca se hubiera ido.” · “Su casa. Nuestra vigilancia.”

08 · Voice

We sound like

  • A calm, senior house manager: short sentences, exact facts.
  • “We checked every door. All clear.”
  • Numbers instead of adjectives: “38 photos, report in 2 hours”.
  • Warm, never cute.

We never

  • Say “security”, “guard”, “patrol” or “protection services” (needs a guard licence in California).
  • Say “realty” or “brokerage” for ourselves; real estate is done by our licensed agent under their broker.
  • Use emoji, exclamation marks or slang in client messages.
  • Promise what a visual inspection cannot see.
09 · Logo rules

Do

  • Use navy or white backgrounds, or a dark photo with enough contrast.
  • Keep the bright m and the quiet letters exactly as designed.
  • Use the shield alone at small sizes.

Don’t

  • Add a roof to the shield, change its shape or rotate it.
  • Recolour the letters (no gold, no all-one-colour except one-colour printing).
  • Stretch, outline, add shadows or effects.
  • Write “Mansionly” in another font next to the logo.
