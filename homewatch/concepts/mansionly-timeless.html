Mansionly: six marks built to last decades

The logos that survive 50+ years (Chase 1961, IBM 1972, Apple 1977, Hermès) follow the same rules. Every mark below is tested against them: on navy, on ivory, printed in one colour like a stamp, and at app and browser-tab size.

Drawn from memory in 5 secondsWorks in one colourReadable at 16 pxNo trendy effectsSymbol can stand alone
T1 · The lit window

Four panes, one is lit: someone is watching the house.

The simplest symbol of home and care. Every client sees their own house in it. Works as an app icon, a sign on a car door, a wax seal.

T2 · The keystone

The stone at the top of an arch that holds the whole house together.

Old-money architecture, and a quiet nod to keys. Feels like a firm that has existed for 100 years.

T3 · Two roofs make an M

A row of houses whose outline is the letter M.

Modern and bold, in the Airbnb spirit, but the shape itself is the oldest one there is: the gable roof.

T4 · The seal

A round seal, like a bank, a university or a wine estate.

Instantly says “established and trusted”. Best for documents, reports, signs and uniforms; paired with T1 or T3 for the app.

T5 · Pure wordmark

Just the name, wide and calm, like the great fashion and jewellery houses.

No symbol to date it. The confidence comes from space and proportion. Icon uses a single M.

T6 · The front door

An arched front door with an M in it: the door we hold the key to.

Warm and classic, readable at any size. Strong as an app icon and on a keyring.

Choose one, or a pair (for example T4 for documents and T1 for the app). I then refine proportions and produce the final files for the site, phone icon, browser tab, social media, business cards and car door.
