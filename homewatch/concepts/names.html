To lock in Mansionward

  1. Check and buy the .com and the .co (Namecheap or GoDaddy, about $10–15 a year).
  2. Search the exact word at tmsearch.uspto.gov in classes 37 and 45.
  3. Check the name and register the LLC at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov (about $70).
  4. File the trademark the same week: $350 per class, or $300–600 through an attorney with a full search. The filing date gives you priority nationwide.
  5. Take the Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google Business Profile names on the same day.

Search done in November 2026 through public web sources. It can miss new filings and small unlisted LLCs.
