Words to keep out of the company name

  • Homewatch: "Homewatch CareGivers" is a national franchise with a registered brand. Using it invites a legal letter and loses you Google searches to them.
  • Realty, Real Estate, Brokers, Properties: in California these words in a name imply a licensed brokerage. Your agent works under their broker, and listings are advertised in the broker's name.
  • Security, Guard, Patrol, Protection, Sentinel: these suggest guard services licensed by the state (BSIS).
  • Trust: restricted in business names in California for anyone who is not a trust company.
  • Legal: legal advice must come from partner attorneys; we coordinate, we don't advise.
  • Before paying for a logo: check the name on USPTO (trademarks), the CA Secretary of State (LLC names) and the .com domain.

After you choose

  1. You pick one name and one concept (or mix: for example C's drawing with A's colors).
  2. Logo: three versions of the mark in the chosen style, plus app icon and favicon.
  3. Full site: about 40 SEO pages (services, neighborhoods, partner pages, guides), English and Spanish.
  4. Client portal with login: visit reports, photos, scanned mail. Staff app for inspectors. Online payments for monthly plans.
  5. Then Google Business Profile, real reviews and partner outreach.
