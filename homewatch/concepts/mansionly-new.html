Mansionly: ten new directions

Nothing here repeats the earlier boards: new symbols (columns, portico, facade, California sun) and new type styles (geometric, poster, slab, heavy didone). Pick by code, for example “R4”.

R1 · The colonnadeAn M built from three columns under a pediment, like the front of a great house.
R2 · Fashion monogramM and L locked together in a ring, the way great fashion houses sign their name.
R3 · California duskThe sun setting behind a roofline on the hills: Southern California, the hour we keep watch.
R4 · The facadeNine windows of a modern house, one is lit. Clean architecture, works at any size.
R5 · Double roofTwo roofs stacked, like a badge of rank: protection on top of protection. Tall poster type.
R6 · The porticoThree arches of a Mediterranean villa. Strong American slab type.
R7 · Key MA solid M whose middle is cut like the teeth of a key.
R8 · Poster wordmarkTall, condensed, confident letters with one gold bar. Reads from across the street.
R9 · Heavy didoneBold magazine-cover serif. Luxury, but with weight, not softness.
R10 · Two buildingsTwo blocks of different heights form an M; the gap between them is a door.
