How it works

Six steps. One year at most.

  1. 01

    Compose

    Choose the length, the links and up to three artifacts. Add your tear or their hair.

  2. 02

    Deposit

    Pay 50%. We issue your cell and its code. Codes exist only with a chain.

  3. 03

    Seal

    Put anything in the cell: words, a film, a photograph. Set the timer, up to one year.

  4. 04

    We forge the chain and string the rest to your length. 3–4 weeks.

  5. 05

    The balance is paid and the key ships, insured. The code is engraved by the clasp.

  6. 06

    Opened

    When the timer ends, the key opens the cell. Not one day before. Not for us.
