How it works

Six steps. One year at most.

  1. 01

    Choose

    Choose the chain and its length. Add a lock of hair or a tear if you want one.

  2. 02

    Deposit

    Pay a 50% deposit. The work begins the day it arrives.

  3. 03

    Made by hand

    Your chain is forged, set and blackened by hand in Los Angeles. 3–4 weeks.

  4. 04

    Seal

    You receive a code. In the Vault you seal a letter: words, a photograph or a film, and the day it opens, up to one year.

  5. 05

    Delivered

    The balance is paid and the chain ships, insured, anywhere in the world. The code is engraved by the clasp.

  6. 06

    Opened

    On the day you chose, the code opens your letter. Not one day before. Not for the wearer, not for you, not for us.

What you receive

  • The chain, forged and blackened by hand, in the length you chose
  • A code engraved by the clasp, hallmarked 925 and with the house mark
  • One letter in the Vault: words, a photograph or a film, sealed for up to a year
  • A black box, a wax seal, a card with your code
  • Repairs of workmanship, for life

The terms
