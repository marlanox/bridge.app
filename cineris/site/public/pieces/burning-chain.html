Burning No. I

Ten chains, each holding ash from one artifact burned unread. Never repeated.

On application

Each Burning edition is built around a single destroyed object. Its ash is divided into ten parts and sealed inside an oxidized silver capsule engraved with the edition numeral. The rest of the chain is yours to compose.

When ten are bound, the edition closes. Read about the Burning.

Specification

  • Ash link: silver capsule with glass window, engraved numeral and number 1–10
  • Remaining links composed with you in the atelier
  • Certificate of provenance of the artifact, held in your Vault cell
  • Invitation to witness the next Burning
