The Vigil

Heavy oxidized cable with a single artifact. The quietest chain we make.

From $1,900

The Vigil is where most members begin. Forty-odd centimetres of forged oval cable in 4.2 mm wire, darkened and rubbed back by hand, interrupted once. You choose the interruption: a micro cameo, a ruby in a closed bezel, a ring of 18k gold, or a reliquary link.

It is heavy enough to fall straight on a black crew-neck and quiet enough to wear every day for twenty years. It is the chain we would give a man who has never worn jewelry.

Specification

  • 925 sterling silver, oxidized and hand-burnished
  • Forged cable, 4.2 mm wire, approximately 9 × 14 mm links
  • One artifact of your choice
  • Hand-chased toggle clasp with house mark, 925 hallmark tag, engraved passport plate
  • One Vault cell, one role in the Order
  • 45 to 70 cm, built to your measurement
