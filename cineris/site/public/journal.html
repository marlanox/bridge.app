  • How to wear a silver chainHow to wear a heavy silver chain, for men and women: with a blazer, a white T-shirt, a black sweater or next to diamonds. Length, layering, and what not to pair it with.

  • Caring for oxidized silverHow to clean oxidized sterling silver without removing the dark finish: dry cloth only, no silver dip, storage, water, perfume and when to have it re-oxidized.
