Terms

Terms of sale

All sales are final. Because every chain is made to order for one person, we do not accept returns or exchanges and do not issue refunds once work has begun.

Made to order

Every chain is assembled by hand after you commission it. A deposit of 50% of the quoted price begins the work. The balance is due before the chain is delivered or collected.

No returns, no exchanges

Because the length, the order of links, the artifacts and anything sealed inside are chosen by you, a chain cannot be resold or remade for anyone else. For that reason we do not accept returns or exchanges, and payments are not refundable once work has begun. You may cancel within 24 hours of paying the deposit, before work starts, for a full refund.

What we do guarantee

Every chain is 925 sterling silver unless described otherwise, and every gold ring is solid 18k. If a chain arrives with a defect in materials or workmanship, we repair or remake it free of charge. We repair workmanship for life (see care & repair). Nothing here limits any right you have under the law where you live.

Materials you give us

Hair, letters, earth or a tear on silk are sealed at your request. We handle them with care, but the house is not liable for loss of personal material beyond the value of the work. Please keep a portion of anything irreplaceable.

The Vault

Each chain includes one Vault cell. Content is stored encrypted and released only on the date set by the person who sealed it. Content must be lawful and must not harm or threaten anyone; we may refuse or remove content that is. If the house ever ceases to operate, we will give at least 90 days’ notice and release every sealed cell to the person who sealed it.

The Order

Roles, missions and suppers are a game played by members. Participation is voluntary and free. Roles are not prizes, have no monetary value, are not transferable, and are not sold. No purchase is necessary to be invited to a supper.

Shipping

Chains ship fully insured with signature required. Customs duties outside the United States are paid by the recipient.
