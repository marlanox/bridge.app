Care & repair

Wear it. That is most of the care.

Oxidized silver is meant to change. The high points get brighter where they touch skin and cloth; the hollows stay dark. After a year your chain will look like yours and nobody else’s.

Do

  • Wipe it with a dry, soft cloth if it gets wet or sweaty.
  • Store it in the black case, closed, away from air.
  • Put it on last and take it off first: after perfume, before sleep.

Do not

  • Never use silver dip or polishing pastes. They strip the oxidation and the chain will look like hardware.
  • Do not swim in chlorine or the sea with it.
  • Do not open the reliquary link. It is sealed; opening it breaks the seal and the glass.

Repair, for life

We repair the workmanship of every chain for as long as the house exists: a stretched link, a loose clasp, a cracked capsule. Bring it to Melrose or ship it insured. We re-oxidize and re-burnish on request for a small fee. Lengthening or adding artifacts later is charged as new work.
