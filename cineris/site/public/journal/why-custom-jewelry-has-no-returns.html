Why custom jewelry has no returns

It was made for one person. It cannot become someone else’s.

Every The Three chain is final sale, and we say so on every page where you could commission one. Here is why, plainly.

It is not stock

Nothing is made before you order it. Your chain is cut to your neck, with links in the order you chose, artifacts you selected, a code engraved for you and, often, your own hair or a tear sealed in glass. There is no second buyer for that chain.

What you are protected by

  • A 24-hour window to cancel after the deposit, before work begins, for a full refund.
  • Repair or remake of any defect in materials or workmanship.
  • Lifetime repair of workmanship.
  • A written description of materials, including stone treatments.

Those promises, and your legal rights, are in the terms.
