How Men Wear a Heavy Silver Chain (Over Cashmere, Not Under It)

The chain goes on the outside. That is the whole decision.

For a long time men wore chains hidden, under the shirt, as if apologising. A heavy oxidized chain is not meant to hide. It is meant to lie on top of good fabric, where its weight and texture can be seen.

Over a black crew-neck

This is the combination the house was built around. A fine black cashmere or merino crew-neck is a dark, matte, soft surface; dark silver with bright high points sits on it like something on velvet in a case. Choose a length of about 55 cm so the chain clears the collar and lies flat on the knit.

With an open shirt

Two buttons open, chain at 50 cm on the collarbone. A white or black shirt both work. Avoid busy prints; the chain is the pattern.

With a T-shirt

Heavy cotton, plain, dark or white. Inside or outside the neckline, both are fine; outside is braver.

What not to do

  • Do not stack three heavy chains. One is a statement, three is a costume.
  • Do not add a pendant. These chains are complete.
  • Do not polish it bright. See why our silver is dark.
