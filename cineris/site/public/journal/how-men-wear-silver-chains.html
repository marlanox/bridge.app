How to Wear a Heavy Silver Chain: Blazer, T-Shirt, Knit, Diamonds

It goes on the outside. That is the whole decision.

For a long time men wore chains hidden, as if apologising. A chain with real weight is not meant to hide. It is meant to be seen, and then forgotten about, because you never take it off.

With a blazer

A navy or black jacket over a T-shirt or a fine knit. The chain at 55 cm sits inside the lapels; the forged section shows on the left, the fine chain almost disappears. This is the most expensive-looking way to wear it.

With a white T-shirt

Heavy cotton, plain. The chain at 50 cm, outside the neckline. Nothing else.

Over a black sweater

Fine cashmere or merino crew-neck, chain at 55–60 cm lying on the knit. Dark on dark, with one gold point.

With diamonds

See wearing dark silver with diamonds.

What not to do

  • Do not add a pendant. These chains are complete.
  • Do not stack three heavy chains. One weight per neck.
  • Do not polish it bright. See why our silver is dark.
