Custom jewelry on Melrose

A bench, black felt, and an hour with no screen.

Melrose Avenue has dressed Los Angeles for decades: punk in the 1980s, vintage and streetwear since, the Sunday flea market on Fairfax. It is a street for people who want things nobody else has. That is why the atelier is here.

What an appointment is like

You sit at the bench. We lay lengths of cable, Balinese and Byzantine chain on black felt and you pick them up, because the decision is about weight, and weight cannot be shown on a website. We measure your neck over the sweater you will wear. We set the order of links together: plain first, then the cameo or the ruby, then texture, then the gold ring.

If the chain is a gift, we help you write the first message for the message and choose the date it opens.

How long it takes

Nine to twenty-one days at the bench. You can come back to watch the reliquary sealed and the last link closed.

How to come

By appointment only. Request an audience or see the atelier.
