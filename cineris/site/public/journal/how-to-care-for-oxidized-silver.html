Caring for oxidized silver

Less is more. Mostly nothing is best.

  • Clean: a dry, soft cotton or microfibre cloth. Nothing else.
  • Never: silver dip, polishing paste, ultrasonic cleaners or toothpaste. They strip the oxidation from the recesses.
  • Water: fine for a moment; dry it afterwards. Avoid chlorine and salt water.
  • Perfume and lotion: put the chain on after, not before.
  • Storage: in its case, closed. Air and humidity change the finish over time.
  • Reliquary link: never try to open it.

When to bring it in

If a link stretches, the clasp loosens, or you want the original contrast restored, send it to the studio. Workmanship repairs are free for life; re-oxidizing costs a small fee. See care & repair.
