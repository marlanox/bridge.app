Time-capsule jewelry

A physical key to a sealed digital letter.

A time capsule is a promise to the future. Most are buried and forgotten. Ours is worn every day, so it is never forgotten, and it cannot be opened early.

How it works

Each chain carries a coded plate engraved with a unique code. That code is the key to one cell in the message. The person who commissions the chain seals text, photographs, audio or video inside and sets the opening date. Until then the wearer sees only a countdown.

Ideas for what to seal

  • A letter to yourself, to open in exactly one year.
  • A recording of a grandparent telling a story, to open at a grandchild’s wedding.
  • Photographs of the day you gave the chain, to open on its tenth anniversary.
  • A confession.

Privacy

Cells are encrypted and the house cannot open them early. If the house ever closed, every sealed cell would be released to the person who sealed it.
