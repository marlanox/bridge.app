Wearing Dark Silver With Diamonds

Dark metal makes white light look whiter.

The old rule kept silver for day and diamonds for night. It never made much sense, and it makes none now that people wear a tennis chain with a T-shirt.

Why it works

Blackened silver absorbs light; a diamond throws it back. Next to oxidized metal, a diamond reads brighter and whiter than it does next to polished platinum. The contrast flatters both.

How to layer

  • With a diamond tennis chain: wear the tennis chain short, at 40–45 cm, and the silver chain longer, at 50–55 cm, so the fine section of ours runs beside the diamonds and the weight falls below them.
  • With studs or a watch: nothing to do. The gold rivet of the Ash Link answers a gold watch; the black answers steel.
  • With a blazer: let the chain sit inside the lapel, over a T-shirt or a fine knit. The weight shows; the fine chain disappears.

Or put the diamond on the chain

Our Old Diamond sets an antique old-cut stone in blackened silver, on the house chain.
