Why our silver is dark

Mirror-polished silver looks like hardware. We wanted something that looks held.

Much of the silver jewelry sold today is polished to a mirror and often plated with rhodium so that it never tarnishes. It is bright, even and permanent. It also looks almost exactly like stainless steel, which costs a tiny fraction as much.

What we do instead

We oxidize every chain to black and burnish it back by hand. The hollows of each link stay dark; the edges glow. Granulation, stamping and the woven Byzantine pattern become legible. In candlelight it looks like old church silver; on black wool it looks like something out of a reliquary.

It changes as you wear it

The parts of the chain you touch most become brighter. After a year it carries a record of how you wear it. A mirror finish records nothing.
