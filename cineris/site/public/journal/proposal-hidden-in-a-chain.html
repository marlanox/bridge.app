A proposal hidden in a chain

Give the chain today. Ask the question in ninety days.

Some of our members have proposed without saying a word. They gave a chain on an ordinary evening, said nothing about the coded plate, and sealed a message in the sealed message that opened weeks later. The message was the question.

How it works

  1. Commission a chain as a gift. You receive a private link to its message cell.
  2. Record the proposal: a letter, a photograph of the ring, a film. Choose the opening date, an anniversary, a birthday, the first day of spring.
  3. Give the chain. Mention, if you like, that it has a cell and that it opens on that day.
  4. On that date, the code code opens the cell.

What people say about it

The wait is the best part. A wearer knows something is sealed and cannot know what. Couples tell us those weeks felt like a long held breath.
