Unisex Silver Chains: One Chain, Any Neck

Weight has no gender.

Most jewelry labelled unisex is men’s jewelry made smaller or women’s jewelry made plainer. We design the other way round: the chain is defined by its weight, its finish and its sequence of links, none of which belongs to anyone.

How men wear them

Single, at 50–55 cm, over a black sweater or an open shirt. See the guide.

How women wear them

Often longer, at 70 cm, doubled into two strands; or a short 45 cm chain worn with a long one. The cameo is often moved to sit at the front.

Couples

Couples sometimes commission the same chain twice at different lengths, or a Concordance pair with passports that open a shared cell.

Ordo Cineris binds every chain by hand on Melrose Avenue, Los Angeles. Build yours in the atelier or request an audience.
