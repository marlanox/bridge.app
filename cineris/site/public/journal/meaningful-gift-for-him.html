A Meaningful Gift for Him: Jewelry That Holds a Message

Most men are hard to buy for because most gifts say nothing.

The hardest person to buy for is usually the one who says he wants nothing. What he means is that he does not want more things. A gift lands when it carries something only you could have given.

Three ways a chain can carry meaning

  • A sealed message. Every chain has a cell in the Vault. Write him a letter, record a video, and set it to open in a year, or on your tenth anniversary. He wears the chain knowing something is waiting.
  • A reliquary. A lock of your hair, earth from the place you met, a line cut from your first letter, sealed in glass in one link.
  • A ring for each year. Start with one 18k gold link. Add one every anniversary.

He will actually wear it

The chains are heavy, dark and without a pendant, made for men who do not wear jewelry. See how men wear them.

Ordo Cineris binds every chain by hand on Melrose Avenue, Los Angeles. Build yours in the atelier or request an audience.
