A meaningful gift for him

Most men are hard to buy for because most gifts say nothing.

The hardest person to buy for is usually the one who says he wants nothing. What he means is that he does not want more things. A gift lands when it carries something only you could have given.

Three ways a chain can carry meaning

  • A sealed message. Every chain has a cell in the message. Write him a letter, record a video, and set it to open in a year, or on your tenth anniversary. He wears the chain knowing something is waiting.
  • A reliquary. A lock of your hair, earth from the place you met, a line cut from your first letter, sealed in glass in one link.
  • A ring for each year. Start with one 18k gold link. Add one every anniversary.
