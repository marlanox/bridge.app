Memorial jewelry with hair

Keeping someone close enough to touch.

Memorial jewelry is one of the oldest uses of jewelry. Victorian mourners wore hair under crystal; earlier generations wore skulls and inscriptions. Today, people ask for the same thing in a form they will actually wear every day.

What can go in the hair link

  • A lock of hair, sealed as it is or tied with thread.
  • A small pinch of cremation ash.
  • A line cut from a letter in their handwriting.
  • Earth or sand from a place that belonged to them.

How it is sealed

The material goes into a capsule of borosilicate glass between silver caps, sealed at the bench, if you wish while you watch. The glass is clear so it can be seen; the seal is permanent.

A message, too

Some families seal a message in the message that opens on the anniversary, written by the whole family. It is a way to say goodbye on a schedule you choose. See the hair link.
