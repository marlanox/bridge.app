Heirlooms

An heirloom for a child

Say the thing now. Let them hear it when they are ready.

Parents rarely say the important things at the right time. They say them too early, when a child cannot understand, or too late. A sealed message solves both problems.

How families use it

  • A father commissions a chain, wears it for years, and seals a letter to open on his daughter’s eighteenth birthday, when the chain becomes hers.
  • A mother seals a video for her son’s wedding day.
  • Parents commission two chains on the same day: one for themselves, one for the child.

Silver that lasts generations

Sterling silver, repaired for life, does not wear out in a lifetime. The oxidized finish ages honestly, so a chain worn by a father arrives in the child’s hands already looking like an heirloom.
