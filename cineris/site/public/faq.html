Questions

What is a Sealed Chain?

A unisex sterling silver chain, made by hand, engraved with a code. Behind the code, in our Vault, is a letter from the person who gave it: words, a photograph or a film. It opens on the day they chose, up to one year later.

How do I buy one?

Choose the chain and length and press Order. We reply within two days with an invoice for a 50% deposit. The work begins when it is paid; the balance is due before shipping.

How much?

From $2,400. Lengths of 60 and 70 cm and a hair or tear link cost extra; the total is shown on each chain.

How long does it take?

3–4 weeks. Every piece is made by hand in Los Angeles.

Can I return it?

No. Every piece is made to order for one person, so all sales are final. Defects are repaired or remade free of charge, and workmanship is repaired for life.

Is it for men or women?

Both. The first chain was a man’s chain worn by a woman. Ours belong to no one gender.

Is it fine jewelry if it is silver?

Yes. Sterling silver is a precious metal; our pieces add solid 18k gold and natural stones, and are made by hand. Fine jewelry is the right name.

How do I seal the letter?

With your order you receive a code. On the Seal page you write the letter, add a photograph or a film if you want, and choose the day it opens, up to one year ahead. It cannot be opened early or changed.

Can I add hair or a tear?

Yes. A lock of hair sealed in glass, or a tear dropped on paper and sealed in resin, set as one link.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes, insured, with signature on delivery.
