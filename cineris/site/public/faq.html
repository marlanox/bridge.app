Questions

What am I buying?

A private cell in our Vault for one year, and the only key to it: a sterling silver chain made by hand, with the cell's code engraved by the clasp. $2,500.

What can I put in the cell?

A letter, a film, a photograph, a voice, a proposal, a confession of love, a last wish, an apology, a secret. Up to 5,000 words of text and one file of up to 50 MB.

Who can open it, and when?

Only whoever holds the key, and only when the timer you set has ended, up to one year. Not before. Not you, not us.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Contents are encrypted. We cannot read them, share them or open them early.

Can I buy only a cell?

No. Cells and codes are issued only with a chain. The chain is the key.

How do I order?

Compose your key and press Order. We reply within two days with an invoice for a 50% deposit and your code. You seal the cell; we make the chain; the balance is paid before shipping.

How long does it take?

3–4 weeks. Every key is made by hand in Los Angeles.

Can I return it?

No. Every key is made to order, so all sales are final. Defects are repaired or remade; workmanship is repaired for life.

Can I add a tear or hair?

Yes: a tear on paper sealed in resin, or a lock of hair sealed in glass, as one bead of the chain.

Is it a will?

No. The Vault keeps words, not property. For an estate, see a lawyer.

Is silver fine jewelry?

Yes. Sterling silver is a precious metal; our keys add solid 18k gold and natural stones and are made by hand.
