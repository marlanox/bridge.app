Ordo Cineris/Questions

Questions

Questions people ask at the door.

Is it real silver?

Yes. Every chain is 925 sterling silver, stamped and tagged with a hallmark. Gold rings are solid 18 karat gold. Rubies are natural. Cameos are carved shell.

Can men wear these chains?

They were designed first for men: heavy, dark, without a pendant, made to lie on a black sweater. Women wear them too, often doubled. They are unisex by construction.

How much does a chain cost?

From $1,900. Most chains are between $2,400 and $3,800 depending on length, links and artifacts. Burning editions are priced on application.

How long does it take?

Nine to twenty-one days at the bench, depending on how many artifacts the chain carries.

Why are there no returns?

Every chain is made for one person to a specification they chose, and many hold personal material. It cannot be sold to anyone else, so all sales are final. We repair workmanship for life.

Can I put my own hair, or a loved one’s, in the chain?

Yes. The reliquary link is a sealed glass capsule for a lock of hair, a tear taken on silk, a line of a letter, or earth from a place. We seal it in front of you.

What is the Vault?

Each chain has a passport code engraved beside the clasp and a sealed cell online. The giver seals a message inside and chooses the day it opens. Until then the wearer can only see how many days remain.

What is the Order?

Every chain is bound with a role drawn from a deck of twelve. Roles carry voluntary missions, and some are called to closed suppers. Roles are never sold.

Do you ship?

Yes, insured and signature-required, in the United States and internationally. Audiences can be held by video.

Where are you?

By appointment on Melrose Avenue, Los Angeles.
