Questions

Is it real silver?

925 sterling silver, hallmarked. Gold is solid 18k. Stones are natural.

How do I recognise a KEPT chain?

Fine chain that turns into forged weight at one black link with a gold point. Weight on the left.

How much?

From $1,800. Most chains are $2,150 to $2,800.

How long?

Nine to twenty-one days.

Returns?

Final sale. Every chain is made for one person. Workmanship repaired for life.

How is a tear kept?

You drop it on blotting paper, it stains the paper, and we seal the paper in clear resin inside a silver link.

How does Voice work?

You record ten seconds. We engrave the waveform on the link and set an NFC chip; tap a phone to hear it.

Where?

By appointment on Melrose Avenue, Los Angeles. Video appointments worldwide.
