Ordo Cineris/The Order

The Order

Twelve roles. You draw one.

At the binding you draw a sealed card from a deck of twelve. Your role is engraved nowhere and written down only in your cell. It can change once a year, at the winter supper.

I

The Witness

You saw something and said nothing. The Order asks you to say it once, to the right person.

Mission
Deliver one sentence you have kept for more than a year.

II

The Ever-Guilty

The role nobody wants and everybody remembers. You carry blame that is not yours, gracefully.

Mission
Grant seven small wishes to seven people who wronged you.

III

The Keeper

You hold a sealed cell for another member. You will never read it. You will know when to hand it over.

Mission
Keep a sealed envelope for one year without opening it.

IV

The Heir

Something will come to you from a member you have never met. Accept it without asking why.

Mission
Receive. Then pass one thing you own to a stranger.

V

The Messenger

You carry a letter across the city by hand. You may not mail it, text it or read it.

Mission
Hand-deliver a letter from the atelier to an address in Los Angeles.

VI

The Debtor

You owe the world something unspecified. You pay it quietly.

Mission
Pay an anonymous stranger’s debt, bill or tab. Tell no one.

VII

The Confessor

At a closed supper, one guest will tell you something true. You take it to the grave.

Mission
Attend the supper you are called to. Listen. Never repeat it.

VIII

The Saint of Small Things

You work in the margins: children, animals, the forgotten.

Mission
Give one full day to a shelter for children or the unhoused.

IX

The Thief of Hours

You take a whole day from someone you love and return it as a gift.

Mission
Plan one day for another person, from waking to sleep.

X

The Mourner

You carry the ash of a Burning. You attend the next one.

Mission
Stand witness at the next Burning and bring one object to give up.

XI

The Stranger

You will be seated at a table where no one knows your name.

Mission
Accept one invitation without asking who else is coming.

XII

The Sovereign

Rare. Drawn perhaps once a year. You choose who is called to the next supper.

Mission
Name the twelve guests of the next closed supper.

The closed suppers

A few times a year the house sets a long table somewhere in Los Angeles and calls certain roles to it: all the Confessors, or one Sovereign and eleven Strangers. Invitations arrive by hand. They cannot be bought, transferred or requested.

The fine print, said plainly

Roles and missions are a game the members play. Every mission is voluntary. Roles have no cash value, are not prizes, and are never sold or offered as a reason to buy. Nobody is ever asked to do anything unsafe, unlawful or against their conscience. Turning a mission down costs nothing.
