What happens at an audience

An audience lasts about an hour. We lay out lengths of chain on black felt and you hold them, because weight is the whole point and a screen cannot tell you about weight. We measure your neck over the sweater you will wear it with. If you are commissioning a reliquary link, bring what goes inside.

If you are commissioning for someone else, we will help you write the first message for the Vault and choose its date. Some people seal it on the spot.

The binding

Most chains are bound in nine to twenty-one days. You may return to watch the final link closed and the reliquary sealed. Many people do.

From out of town

We bind for clients outside Los Angeles by video audience. Chains ship insured and signature-required inside a black lacquered case.
