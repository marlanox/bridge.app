Chain lengths for men

Where a chain falls changes what it says.

A chain’s length decides where it lands on the body, and where it lands decides how it reads. These are guides for an average neck; we measure yours in the studio.

  • 45 cm / 18 in. Collar. Sits at the base of the neck. Visible with almost any neckline; can feel tight on a large neck.
  • 50 cm / 20 in. Classic. Lies on the collarbone, framed by an open shirt.
  • 55 cm / 22 in. Over the knit. Falls clear of a crew-neck collar and lies on the sweater. Our most requested length.
  • 60 cm / 24 in. Long. On the upper chest. Good over a turtleneck.
  • 70 cm / 28 in. Very long. Can be doubled.

Weight changes length

A heavy chain hangs straighter and lower than a light chain of the same length. If in doubt with a massive chain, go one size shorter.

Measure yourself

Lay a piece of string around your neck over the sweater you will wear the chain with, mark where you want it to fall, and measure the string. Then build to that length.
