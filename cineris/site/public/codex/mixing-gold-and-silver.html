Mixing gold and silver

One warm thing in a cold composition.

The old rule said never mix gold and silver. It was a rule for matching sets, and matching sets are dead. Mixing works perfectly well when one metal clearly leads and the other is an accent with a reason to be there.

The rule of one

In our chains silver leads absolutely: fifty centimetres of it, darkened. Gold appears in every chain exactly once, as the point at the centre of the black Mark, and at most once more, as a single ring of solid 18k. Two warm points draw the eye and the thumb. Three make a pattern, and a pattern is decoration rather than an event.

Why 18 karat

18k is 75% gold. It is richer in colour than 14k and next to dark silver it looks almost orange, which is the contrast we want. It is also soft enough to show wear honestly over the years.

Wearing it with other pieces

If you wear a gold watch, the gold link answers it. If you wear a silver ring, the chain answers that. Either way the chain belongs.
