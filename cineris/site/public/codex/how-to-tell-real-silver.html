How to tell real silver

Stamps can be faked. Physics cannot.

  • Look for marks. 925, STERLING or STER plus a maker’s mark. A quality mark without a maker’s mark is a warning sign in the US.
  • Magnet. Silver is not magnetic. If a strong magnet grabs the chain, it is steel or nickel underneath.
  • Tarnish. Real silver darkens over time. Steel and rhodium-plated chains stay mirror-bright forever.
  • Weight. Silver is dense. A massive-looking chain that feels light is probably hollow or plated.
  • Ice. Silver conducts heat extremely well; ice placed on it melts unusually fast.
  • Acid test. A jeweler can test a hidden spot with silver testing solution. Ask before they do it on oxidized pieces.

Red flags

“Silver tone,” “silver plated,” “German silver” (which contains no silver) and “stainless” are not sterling. Prices far below the metal value are not bargains.
