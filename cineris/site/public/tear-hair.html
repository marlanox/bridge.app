Tear & Hair

One bead of your chain can hold a tear, or the hair of the one you love. It stays there for life.

A tear · +$450

  1. We send you a small black envelope with a square of blotting paper.
  2. You let one tear fall on it. Where it lands, the paper changes colour.
  3. You send it back in the envelope we included.
  4. We cut the stained paper and seal it in clear resin inside a silver bead.
  5. The bead becomes one link of your chain.

A lock of hair · +$560

  1. Cut a lock about 5 cm long and as thick as a pencil. Tie one end with thread first.
  2. Put it in the envelope we send you, or bring it to us.
  3. We seal it in clear glass between two silver caps.
  4. The bead becomes one link of your chain.

Keep a portion of anything irreplaceable.
