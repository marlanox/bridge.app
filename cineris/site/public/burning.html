The Burning

One irreplaceable object. One fire. 10 chains that hold what is left. When the last is bound, the edition closes and is never made again.

Applications open

No. I: The Unopened Note

A folded note from inside a Victorian mourning locket, inscribed not to be opened until I am forgiven. Burned unread in front of witnesses. Its ash is sealed in the ash link of each chain, engraved with the numeral I and the chain’s number from 1 to 10.

Each Burning chain is built to its owner’s length and order of links, around the ash link. Each carries the passport plate, a Vault cell, and a role from the Order.

How a Burning works

  1. The artifact. The house acquires one object that cannot be replaced: an unsent letter, a wedding photograph, a page of a diary, a contract never signed. Its provenance is recorded and kept in the Vault.
  2. The witnesses. The Burning happens at night in the atelier, in front of the members who hold the role of Mourner, and whoever the edition’s first owners invite.
  3. The ash. The ash is divided by weight into 10 equal parts and sealed into glass inside a blackened silver capsule.
  4. The 10 chains. Each owner chooses the rest of their chain in the atelier. The ash link is the only part that is the same in all of them.
  5. The close. When the last chain is bound, the edition is closed and announced. No Burning is ever repeated.

Future Burnings

Members may propose an artifact for a future Burning. Most proposals are declined. The ones that are accepted are usually the ones that were hardest to give up.
