The Legend

  1. I

    Revenge

    In 2011, Marla Nox opened her first jewelry house and called it Revenge. She was young enough to believe revenge was something you do.

  2. II

    Paris

    She trained as a designer in France, then spent ten years inside the fashion weeks with 8 Billion People and The Three. Ten years of watching what people show, and what they hide.

  3. III

    The ingot

    At the end, she melted what was left of Revenge into a single bar of silver. A sliver of it is cast into every piece the house makes. Revenge is still in there. It just does not speak.

  4. IV

    Nox

    Her name means night. The house works at night, and shows its pieces the way they were made: one at a time, in the dark.

  5. V

    Withheld

    Revenge is what you do. Withheld is what you keep.

The house rules

  1. We never say who wears us.
  2. Every piece withholds something: a lock of hair, a tear, a message sealed for up to a year, or only the name of the one who gave it.
  3. Each piece is made for one neck.
  4. We do not explain.
  5. Nothing comes back. Not pieces, not secrets.
