The Legend

  1. I

    The letter

    In the winter of 2011, Marla Nox had a jewelry house called Revenge, and a man who wore the first chain she ever made. The night he left, he took the chain off, wrapped it around a sealed envelope and closed her hand over both. On the envelope he had written three words. Not for one year.

  2. II

    The year

    She did not open it. She wore his chain instead, under her collar, every day for three hundred and sixty-five days. It was too long for her and too heavy, and she never took it off. She trained in France. She walked into ten years of fashion weeks. She made clothes for strangers. The envelope stayed shut.

  3. III

    Three sentences

    On the last night of the year she opened it. Inside were three sentences. She has never told anyone what they were. She says only that after them, revenge was impossible.

  4. IV

    The Three

    She closed Revenge. She kept the chain. Everything she has made since carries the name of that night: The Three. First the clothes. Then the silver.

  5. V

    The rule

    From that night the house has one rule. Every chain we make carries a letter that waits. Never longer than a year, because she waited a year, and that was enough.

  6. VI

    The chain

    Every chain is cut from his: a man’s chain, worn by whoever it was given to. That is why ours belong to no one gender. Silver, blackened by hand. A clasp engraved with a code. Behind the code, in our vault, a letter that no one can read before its day. Not the one who wears it. Not the one who sent it. Not us.

You will understand the three sentences when your own letter opens.
